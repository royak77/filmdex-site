Delete your Filmdex account

Filmdex · developed by Rumble Rock Media

Delete it in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Filmdex and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings (top-right of your Profile).
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account.
  4. Type DELETE and confirm.

Your account and data are deleted immediately.

Can't access the app?

Email nroyak@gmail.com from the address you signed up with (or include your Filmdex @username) and ask for your account to be deleted. We'll confirm and delete it within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account, profile, username and profile photo
  • Your rankings, showdowns, ratings and ranking history
  • Your Watch History, Blind Spots, Rewatch and Not Interested lists, and check-ins
  • Your quests, friends and friend requests, likes, comments, Snapshots and polls
  • Your notification and app settings

What's kept

  • Group movie nights you took part in stay in the other participants' history, with your name replaced by "Former member".

Your usage-analytics profile at our analytics provider (PostHog) is deleted within 30 days of your account deletion. Nothing that identifies you is kept after that. See our Privacy Policy.
