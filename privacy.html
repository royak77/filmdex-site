Filmdex Privacy Policy

Effective September 28, 2026 · Filmdex is developed by Rumble Rock Media

Filmdex is an app for ranking the films you've seen, tracking what you want to watch, and comparing taste with friends. This policy explains what information Filmdex collects, how it's used, who it's shared with, and the choices you have. Questions: filmdex.app@gmail.com.

Information you give us

  • Account information: your email address, and your name if you sign in with Apple or Google. You choose a display name and a unique @username.
  • Profile: an optional profile photo and bio.
  • Your film activity: films you've seen, your rankings and showdown choices, Blind Spot / Rewatch / Not Interested lists, check-ins, quests, and imports you choose to upload (for example a Letterboxd or IMDb CSV).
  • Social content: friend requests and friendships, comments, likes, Snapshots, polls, and movie-night sessions you join.

Information collected automatically

  • Usage analytics: which screens and features you use and when, plus crash and error reports, collected with PostHog and linked to your account so we can understand what's working and fix problems.
  • Device information: device type, operating system version, app version, and an analytics device identifier.
  • Push notification token, if you allow notifications, so we can send you alerts like friend requests.

Filmdex does not collect your precise location, contacts, or advertising ID, and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

How we use it

  • To run the app: your account, rankings, lists, recommendations and social features.
  • To show your profile, rankings and activity to your friends, and your @username, display name, photo and showdown counts to other Filmdex users (for example on the Global leaderboard or in search).
  • To send notifications you've allowed.
  • To understand usage, fix bugs, and improve Filmdex.

We don't sell your personal information, and Filmdex has no ads.

Who we share it with

Only service providers that run parts of Filmdex on our behalf:

The Movie Database (TMDB)Film information and posters. Your searches for films are sent to TMDB; no account information is.

We may also disclose information if required by law.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your account and all of the data above are deleted immediately from our database, and your analytics profile is deleted within 30 days. Group movie nights you took part in remain in the other participants' history with your name replaced by "Former member".

Your choices

  • Delete your account anytime in the app: Settings → Delete Account. Or see how to request deletion.
  • Export your data from Settings.
  • Edit your profile, photo and username anytime.
  • Turn off notifications in your device settings.
  • For any other request about your data (access, correction or deletion), email filmdex.app@gmail.com.

Security

All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and access to your data is restricted to your own account and, for the parts you share, your friends.

Children

Filmdex is not intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date at the top, and tell you in the app for significant changes.
